About the Archivist

Greetings. I am Calvin Slaven, Keeper of the Archives, Guardian of Questionably Accurate Zelda Lore, and Consumer of Excessive Quantities of Mountain Dew.

For years, I have been cataloging the vast and increasingly convoluted history of Hyrule. My quest began not with the official timeline—no, I sought to understand the deeper mysteries that Nintendo left ambiguous. Why does the timeline split? What happened between each game? And most importantly: where did all these Maps and Timelines come from?

"The greatest treasure in all of Hyrule is not the Triforce or the Master Sword. It's a properly indexed spreadsheet."

This archive was created to serve as a beacon for fellow Zelda enthusiasts, speedrunners, lore hunters, and anyone who has ever wondered why there are three separate timelines. If you've scrolled through forums at 2 AM debating whether the Kingdom of Hyrule exists in a flat plane or a spherical realm, this place is for you.

The Hyrule Archives is my contribution to preserving the legacy of one of gaming's greatest franchises—complete with interactive timelines, hidden easter eggs (if you know where to look), and a sincere belief that someday, Nintendo will release a game that canonically clarifies everything we've been arguing about.

May your wifi be strong and your discoveries legendary.

— Calvin Slaven, Archivist
